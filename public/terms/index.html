What this is

Big Sky Uber (“we”, “us”) is a fictional, player-run ride business inside the Big Sky private server for Emergency Response: Liberty County on Roblox. This website lets members of that community request in-game rides, and lets our drivers and staff manage them.

We are not Uber Technologies Inc., and we have no connection to it, to Roblox Corporation, or to the developers of ER:LC. No real-world transportation is offered. All prices are in in-game currency.

Accounts

You sign in with Discord. You’re responsible for what happens on your account. Don’t share it or sign in as somebody else. You must meet Discord’s and Roblox’s minimum age requirements to use this site.

Rides & fares

  • Only request rides you actually intend to take, and be in-game at your pickup.
  • Use your exact Roblox username so your driver can find you.
  • Fares are paid in in-game cash when you arrive. The driver sets the final fare inside your class’s published range.
  • We never ask for real money, Robux, gift cards, passwords or personal information.
