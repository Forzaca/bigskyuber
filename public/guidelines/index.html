Rider & driver guidelines

Good roleplay makes every ride better. Here’s how we keep Big Sky Uber smooth for everyone.

For ridersPayingFor driversRoleplayReportsStrikes

For riders

  • Be in-game and at your pickup when you request. Add a postal code if you can.
  • Use your exact Roblox username, so your driver knows who to pick up.
  • One active ride at a time. Cancel if your plans change.
  • Treat your driver’s car with respect. No ramming, no jumping out mid-trip.
  • Keep notes useful: “outside the gas station, red jacket” beats “hurry”.

Paying

  • Fares are in in-game cash and paid on arrival.
  • Your driver sets the fare inside your class’s range. Uber Black is always $500 – $1,000.
  • Skipping a fare is treated the same as a no-show.

For drivers

  • Only go online when you are actually in-game and able to take rides.
  • Accept one ride at a time and update its status as you go: on the way, arrived, on trip, completed.
  • Charge inside the published range. Never ask for anything outside the game.
  • Only take Premium or Black rides if you are approved for that class and driving a suitable car.
  • Go offline when you leave. The site will also mark you offline automatically after a few hours.

Roleplay

Big Sky Uber is a civilian business. Follow the server’s roleplay rules. Don’t use rides to dodge police scenes, and don’t use the service to set up FRP or RDM. Staff decisions in-game come first.

Reports

Something went wrong? Open a ticket in the Big Sky Discord with the ride ID. Staff can see the full timeline of every ride.

Strikes

Repeated no-shows, unpaid fares, harassment or abuse of the site can lead to a ban from requesting rides, posting reviews or driving. Serious cases may be passed to server staff.
