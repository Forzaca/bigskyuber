Questions

Paying for your ride.

How do I pay?

In-game cash, handed to your driver when you reach your destination. Big Sky Uber never takes real money, Robux, or anything outside the game.

Who decides the final fare?

Your driver, inside your class’s range. Short local hops sit near the minimum, long county-wide trips near the maximum. The estimate you see when booking is a good guide.

What if I can’t pay?

Don’t book a ride you can’t afford. Skipping a fare is a breach of the rider guidelines and can get you banned from requesting rides.

Can I tip?

Tips are always welcome in-game, but never expected. They don’t change your recorded fare.

Why is Uber Black so much more?

Uber Black is our executive tier: black cars only, our best-rated drivers, and door-to-door service. It’s priced at $500–$1,000 per trip to match.
