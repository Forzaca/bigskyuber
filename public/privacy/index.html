Privacy Policy

We keep as little as we can: enough to run rides and show reviews. We never sell anything, and we never ask for real-world personal details.

What we collect

  • From Discord when you sign in: your user ID, username, display name and avatar. If the server is linked, we also read which roles you have in the Big Sky Discord server, only to decide if you can open the driver or admin panels. We never see your email, password or messages.
  • When you request a ride: your Roblox username, in-game pickup and drop-off locations and postal codes, ride class, number of passengers and any notes you write.
  • When you drive: your Roblox username, in-game vehicle, callsign, online status and ride totals.
  • When you review: your rating, your text and which ride it was for.

Everything about rides is in-game roleplay information. Please don’t put real-world personal details in any field.

How we use it

To match riders with drivers, show your ride status, keep a ride log for staff, show reviews, prevent abuse (for example rate limits and bans), and post ride updates to staff channels in our Discord.

Who sees it

  • Your driver sees your Discord name, Roblox username, locations and notes for your ride.
  • Staff with the admin role see the full ride log, driver list and sign-in list.
  • The ER:LC server API: if enabled, we check your Roblox username against the live player list of the Big Sky ER:LC server when you request a ride. Nothing is sent to ER:LC. We only read the list.
  • The Big Sky Discord bot can read ride logs, reviews and online drivers through our private API, to post them in the server.
  • Everyone can see published reviews (your display name and avatar) and which drivers are online.

We don’t sell or share data with advertisers. There are no ads or third-party trackers on this site.

Cookies

We use one signed sign-in cookie to keep you logged in for up to 7 days, and a short-lived cookie during Discord sign-in. Your browser may also remember your Roblox username locally to pre-fill the ride form.

Where it’s stored

The site runs on Vercel. Data is stored in Vercel Blob storage. Avatars load from Discord’s servers.

How long

We keep the most recent rides and reviews (older ones roll off automatically) and the most recent sign-ins. Ban records are kept until the ban is lifted.

Removal

Want your rides, reviews or account details removed? Open a ticket in the Big Sky Discord from the account in question and staff will delete them.

Younger users

You must meet Discord’s minimum age to sign in. We don’t knowingly collect anything beyond what’s listed above from anyone.
