Fair

Help & FAQ

Good questions.

Is this the real Uber?

No. Big Sky Uber is a fictional business inside a Roblox ER:LC roleplay server. It isn’t affiliated with Uber Technologies Inc. Nothing here involves real cars, real money or real-world rides.

What do I need to request a ride?

A Discord account to sign in, your exact Roblox username, and to be in-game at your pickup location. Pick an ER:LC landmark like Civilian Spawn (202) or the Shopping Outlet (403) and we’ll fill in the postal for you.

How long until a driver accepts?

Usually under a minute when drivers are online. You can see how many are on duty on the home page. If nobody is on, your request waits in the queue.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time before your trip starts, from the ride page. Please don’t book rides you don’t intend to take.

My driver didn’t show up / something went wrong.

Open a ticket in the Big Sky Discord with your ride ID (it looks like BSU-ABC123). Staff can see the full ride log.

How do I become staff or a driver?

Apply in the Discord server. Once you’re given the driver role you can sign in at /workers. Admins use /admin.
